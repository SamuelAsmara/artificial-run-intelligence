fix: one avatar everywhere, a photo editor that actually moves, and a week
strip for the coach's plan

FOUR THINGS, THREE OF THEM THE SAME BUG WEARING DIFFERENT CLOTHES.

The photo editor felt frozen, and it was. It stored the whole image plus an
`object-position` and let you drag that around — but `object-fit: cover`
only leaves something to pan along the axis where the image overflows its
box. Every photo from a phone is a portrait, so inside a square circle it
overflows vertically and not at all horizontally: dragging sideways did
literally nothing and dragging up and down ran out after a centimetre.
There was no bug in the drag handler. The mechanism could not do the job.

Now it pans *and zooms*, and what is saved is a square crop of exactly what
the circle shows. Zoom is what creates room to pan — at 1.4x a portrait
overflows both ways. Storing the crop also retires a whole class of
disagreement: every box that shows an avatar is square, so there is no
framing rule left to get wrong. `profiles.avatar_position` stays for the
rows written before this and stops being consulted.

The activity page showed a name where a photo belongs. Five components had
grown their own avatar, each falling back differently when there was no
photo: to a file that does not exist in `public/`, to a blue smiley, to the
literal word "Photo", and — on the activity page — to bare initials with no
circle around them, which reads as a caption rather than a portrait. One
`Avatar` now, with initials that scale with the circle so they look
deliberate at 32px and at 116px, and an `onError` so a photo that cannot be
decoded never leaves a broken-image icon.

Clicking an avatar enlarges it. A plain fixed overlay rather than a
`<dialog>`: it has to close on Escape, on the backdrop and on the button,
and it must never be able to trap the page.

The coach's plan was two flat lists — every upcoming session as a full row
with a Change button, then every past one underneath. On a fourteen-week
plan that is ninety-eight expanded rows and finding Thursday means
scrolling. It is now a week of seven squares, which is the vocabulary the
athlete already sees on their dashboard and plan screen, so coach and
athlete look at the same shape. One session opens at a time, underneath the
day it belongs to, with arrows across the weeks. The editor also says out
loud what migration 0014 made true: a session you change is yours, and the
adjustment engine will leave it alone.

Deleted `ImageSlot`, which had no callers left.

554 tests pass, 4 of them new. tsc clean. next build passes.
