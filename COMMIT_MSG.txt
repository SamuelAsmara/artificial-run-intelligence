feat(billing): coach and athlete packages; README for submission

A coach picks a package right after signing up, on /upgrade: Basic is
free and stops at five athletes, Premium is unlimited and will carry the
free-text chat. The choice is stored in subscriptions, shown in coach
settings against the roster ("3 of 5 athletes"), and the Basic limit is
enforced where it has to be — inside join_coach (migration 0022), since
the athlete redeeming a code cannot read the coach's subscription. No
payment provider is connected; Premium opens without a charge and the
page says so.

Athletes get the same two packages on the settings screen: Basic is free
and is the whole product; Premium (RunAI, free-text chat) is shown as
"coming next" with no button, so nothing on the page looks like a
purchase. Migration 0023 gives subscriptions a scope column so a coach
who also trains holds one row per role.

Email: Supabase Auth sends the emails, Resend is the SMTP provider —
email/README.md has the dashboard settings and the branded confirmation
template. README gains a "For Reviewers" section with the two demo
accounts and notes the Resend test-domain limitation.

docs/: the working notes that no longer describe the product (plans and
gap maps from 19 Aug, the design port log, the first markdown drafts and
the ARI-era archive) are removed; the audit and QA records stay, and the
progress log is brought up to date. The six documents and the
presentation follow in a separate commit.
