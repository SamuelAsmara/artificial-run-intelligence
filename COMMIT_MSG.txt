feat: Ask ARI, and fix the coach screens by looking at them

Ask ARI replaces the mocked chat on the dashboard. The chat cycled three
canned replies over a scripted exchange whose every number belonged to the
prototype's athlete, and it was gated !isReal so it could never appear on a
real dashboard - a feature that could not run, left in the file to be read.

What replaces it answers ten questions from the athlete's own runs: how much
they have improved this month, whether they are ramping too fast, whether
they stuck to the plan, how much of their training is easy, whether cardiac
drift is improving. Every figure comes from a pure function with a test
behind it, so nothing can be invented.

Deliberately a list of questions rather than a text box. A text box promises
"ask me anything", and the first thing it cannot parse reads as broken rather
than as mostly working. The filter above the list narrows these questions and
never accepts free text, so everything on screen answers. One fetch, on first
open, so an athlete who never asks loads none of it.

Every question declines honestly when it has too little data, and there is a
test for that case on each of them.

Also in this commit:

- classify() and the median pace it reads move out of app/activities/page.tsx
  into lib/activity/classify.ts with tests. It was arithmetic living in a
  page, which is where all nine defects of the aggregation audit were found
  and where none of the tested modules had any. The activities list and the
  questions now share one definition of what a tempo run is.
- two charts were scaled from zero and hid what they were drawn to show:
  fourteen days of a load ratio between 0.8 and 1.4 became fourteen identical
  bars. They now scale to their own range, print the floor, and say so.
- coach screens, found by rendering them rather than by testing them: cycle
  tiles overflowed their container, the athlete table had no column headers,
  a stat tile was handed a whole sentence in a 25px face, and a chart caption
  collided with the top gridline.
- filter labels on the roster sat on two different baselines, one paragraph
  repeated once per template, and the empty dashboard's header was ordered
  differently from the populated one - so an athlete's first sync rearranged
  the page.
- slide 7 of the deck now carries a real screenshot of the coach board.

809 tests pass, up from 746.

Plan screen: a calendar you page through, and the arc of the plan.

The plan page was an accordion of every week stacked on one screen; a
sixteen-week plan was sixteen cards to scroll past. It is now a real
calendar: one month at a time, previous/next, a Today shortcut, and
tapping a date opens the same session detail the week view used to show.
On phones the seven-column grid collapses to date + distance cells (the
type edge still carries the kind) instead of overflowing off the screen.

Fixed in passing: the month grid started weeks on Monday while the whole
product - the dashboard calendar, the generator's Friday-long-run and
Saturday-rest placement, lib/time/week itself - starts them on Sunday,
so every month view drew each day one column off. Sunday-first now.

New: the phase timeline. generatePlan has computed a phase (base/build/
peak/taper) for every workout since day one, and generatePlanAction
dropped it on the exact line that wrote rows to the database. Migration
0020 adds plan_workouts.phase, the action now stores it, and the plan
screen draws a segmented timeline - proportions the generator or the
coach's template actually decided - with a marker on the current week.
Plans built before the migration have no phases and the bar hides
itself rather than inventing an arc; rebuilding a plan brings it back.

809 tests pass.
