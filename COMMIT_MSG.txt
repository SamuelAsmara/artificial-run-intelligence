fix the coach board's silent truncation, mark record runs, and stop dropping distance

The twenty-athlete demo dataset did what it was built to do: it surfaced a
class of bug that cannot exist with one account.

getCoachWorkspace read a roster's worth of sessions with no range and no
order. PostgREST returns at most a thousand rows and says nothing about the
rest, and the window holds about fourteen hundred. The board showed 32
sessions in a week where there were 80, eight on a Thursday where there were
twenty, and left the athlete with the most missed sessions off the "needs
you" list entirely, because his rows were among the ones dropped. Nothing
errored. The screen was simply wrong, and looked fine.

Two neighbouring queries were one growth step from the same fault and only
survived by accident: readiness_snapshots read 2,162 rows to keep 20, and
activities read every run the roster had ever logged. The first is now
bounded by a row count, the second by the date window — deliberately not by
a row count, because a run outside the window cannot mark a session inside
it, and truncating would have invented missed sessions. The per-athlete
filter became a Map; it was 28,000 comparisons per render and grew with the
product of roster and window.

comparePlanned computed a distance note and then threw it away whenever the
pace verdict fired. An athlete given 6.6 km who ran 15.7 was told only that
his pace was 20 s/km slow. Past 25% drift the distance now leads the note;
below it, it trails. The verdict tag still follows pace, because pace is
what the plan prescribed.

Adds recordSetters: which runs broke a record on the day they were run,
rather than which runs hold a record today. A personal best is a day, not a
standing title — a run keeps its mark after it has been beaten. The list
draws it with the gold token globals.css already reserves for personal
records, as an inset ring so the row does not move by a pixel.

The seed was writing streams_fetched_at with no pace_shape or best_efforts,
which was worse than writing neither: the app concluded the streams had been
fetched and were empty, so every chart said there was no detail and every
personal best showed a dash. Both columns are now generated per session
type.

565 tests, 11 of them new.
